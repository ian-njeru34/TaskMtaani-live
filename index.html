Helper Applications

Review applicants before creating a provider account. Approval gives the applicant provider access, but does not automatically mark them verified or active.

ApplicantProfessionExperienceAreaStatusSubmittedAction
Connect Supabase to load applications.

Approval workflow

  1. Review the applicant's profession, experience, area and availability.
  2. Confirm identity and references using your normal verification process.
  3. Approve the application to create/link the provider profile.
  4. Use Helpers to set verification status and active status after checks are complete.
  5. Assign the provider to bookings from the Bookings area.

Provider-to-Booking Assignment

Assign pending or confirmed bookings to active, verified helpers who offer the requested service.

BookingServiceDateLocationCurrent HelperAssign HelperStatus
Connect Supabase to load bookings.

Assignment rules

  • Only active and verified helpers are offered for assignment.
  • Helpers must have an active provider-service entry matching the booking service.
  • Assignment is controlled by administrators.
  • Assigning a helper changes the booking status to assigned.
  • Changing or removing an assignment does not change the helper's verification status.
